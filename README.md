## ⚡ Instant Deployment (GitHub Pages)

You only need **one file**: [`index.html`](index.html).

1. **Upload `index.html`** to your GitHub repository root.
2. In your repository on GitHub:
   - Click **Settings** &rarr; **Pages**.
   - Under **Build and deployment**, set Source to **Deploy from a branch** (`main` / root).
   - Click **Save**.
3. Your live classroom is immediately available at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`.

## 🚀 How Teachers and Students Connect

### 1. For the Professor / Teacher:
1. Open the GitHub Pages URL in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari.
2. Click **Host as Teacher**, enter your name, and click **Launch Session**.
3. A unique **6-Digit Session Code** (e.g. `629402`) is generated.
4. Click **Start Presentation** to select and broadcast your screen with system audio.
5. When students join, a prominent **Floating Admission Bar** appears at the top of your screen:
   - Click **[ ✓ Admit Student ]** to grant immediate access.
   - Click **[ ✕ Decline ]** to decline access.

### 2. For Students:
1. Open the same URL on any laptop, tablet, or phone.
2. Enter the teacher's **6-Digit Session Code** and your **Display Name**.
3. Click **Enter Classroom Stage**.
4. The student enters the **Admission Request Submitted** waiting screen with the Amrita emblem and animated loader.
5. Once the professor clicks **Admit**, the student immediately enters the live classroom!
6. Full 1080p 60fps screen presentation, real-time group chat, raised hands, and floating emoji reactions stream with zero latency!

- **Screen & System Audio Sharing**: Ultra-low latency 1080p 60fps streaming directly from professor to students.
